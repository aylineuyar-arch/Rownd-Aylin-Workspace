                Architecture

                1. 1

                  Pick a topic. Paste an RFI manually, or pull one live from the DoD SBIR/STTR feed.

                2. 2

                  Generate a draft. The Generation Engine assembles a prompt from the topic, the Template Library, and any proven wording Trend Analysis has surfaced, then calls Claude Fable and/or Gemini.

                3. 3

                  Review & edit. Compare drafts from each model, edit sections, regenerate as needed.

                4. 4

                  Export. Download the finished draft as a Word doc or Google Doc.

                5. 5

                  Tag the outcome. Once you hear back, mark the submission won, lost, or no reply in Historic Submissions.

                6. 6

                  Learn from it. That outcome lands in the Memory Store, and Trend Analysis mines it for wording that correlates with wins — it'll show up highlighted in your next draft.

                7. +

                  Tentative: a Topic Poller could run step 1 automatically, on a schedule, instead of waiting for you to check.
